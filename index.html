Why Soft Washing

Pressure alone damages roofs. Chemistry does the work.

Soft washing is a cleaning method using low pressure and specialized solutions to safely clean your roof. Instead of blasting the surface, we treat what is actually growing on it — so the stains leave and the shingles stay put.

  1. 01

    Free on-site quote

    We look at the actual surface, identify what is growing there, and give you a straight price. No pressure, no surprises.

  2. 02

    Protect the property

    Plants, landscaping and fixtures get covered and rinsed. We work around your home the way we would work around our own.

  3. 03

    Treat, dwell, rinse

    Specialized solutions are applied at low pressure, given time to kill the algae, mold, mildew, lichen and moss, then gently rinsed.

  4. 04

    Walk it with you

    We finish by checking the work together, so you see the difference before we pack up and invoice.
